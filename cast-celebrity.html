Chapter III

Cast & Celebrity Influence

How much did the film’s famous cast help people notice The Odyssey?

Overview

The Odyssey has one of the most famous casts in recent film, including Matt Damon, Tom Holland, Zendaya and Anne Hathaway. We looked at three places to see how much attention the cast drew: TikTok, Google searches and X.

Each place shows something different. TikTok shows what people watch, Google shows what people look up, and X shows what people argue about.

Our sample was 400 TikTok videos about four of the stars, Google search trends for six cast members, and 10 posts from X.

1. Attention hit all at once, around release

Interest in the cast stayed low for most of the year, then spiked in release week.

Google searches for every cast member we tracked reached their highest point in the same week: release week in July 2026. Before that, interest was low and flat. TikTok shows the same pattern. Of the 391 million views, 171 million came in the two weeks before release and 145 million in the two weeks after.

What this shows: Most of the attention came in a few weeks: the two weeks before release and the two after drew 81% of all views. After August the conversation was mostly over.

2. Zendaya stood out most

Zendaya led TikTok in views and was named in 9 of the 10 X posts we sampled.

Zendaya’s videos earned 139 million views, more than any other star. Most of that came from premiere and red-carpet fashion content (about 90 million views) and from the two weeks before release, when she was on the press tour.

3. Stars together drew more than stars alone

Videos that named exactly two of the four stars did far better than videos that named one.

We counted how many of the four stars each video named in its caption and looked at the typical (median) number of views. The strongest pairing was Tom Holland and Zendaya, with 56 videos and 71 million views.

4. TikTok showed spectacle, and Google showed curiosity

People watched fashion and fan videos on TikTok, but looked up facts and surprises on Google.

The biggest TikTok topic was red-carpet and premiere fashion: 94 videos and 147 million views, 38% of everything in the sample. Fan edits came second and had the highest rate of likes, comments and shares. Only about 7 videos (0.4% of views) were clearly negative.

Google also caught a surprise that TikTok and X missed: Travis Scott’s searches jumped because people were asking whether he was really in the film, a sign that his casting surprised many.

5. Who ranks first depends on where you look

The cast looks different on each platform, and only Zendaya was near the top on all of them.

Each star’s rank on TikTok and on Google

1 = most attention. TikTok rank is by total views. Google rank is by average weekly searches.

What this shows: The order changes between platforms. Zendaya is first on TikTok and second on Google. Tom Holland is first on Google but third on TikTok. Matt Damon falls from second to fourth. Watching and searching are different kinds of attention.

Google scores each person against their own peak, so only the order is meaningful, not the size.

6. The director’s name carried weight too

Videos that named Christopher Nolan earned a large share of the views.

Nolan was named in 108 of the 400 cast videos, which earned 102 million views. In a separate sample of 87 TikTok videos from a general “odyssey” search, 21 videos named him. In 15 of those 21, though, his name appeared only as a hashtag, not in the written caption.

Things to keep in mind

  • Google scores are scaled to each person’s own peak week, so they show rank order, not who had more searches. The Google data covers the U.S. only.
  • The X sample is only 10 posts. It gives a hint, not a measurement.
  • We tagged TikTok topics and names with keyword rules, which can miss context. We counted captions and numbers, not what viewers wrote in comments.
  • The videos come from searches for each star’s name plus “odyssey,” so they reflect those searches, not all of TikTok. The wider Nolan sample included unrelated videos, such as car listings and video games.
